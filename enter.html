Breathe

A quiet space to slow down and let the thoughts you haven’t said out loud rise and pass. Take a breath, put feelings into words, and release them without needing to make them perfect.

A note about privacy: Breathe thoughts are not saved or encrypted—they’re simply sent into the void. Please don’t include personal or sensitive information.

Whisper

Send a random thought into a shared space. Passersby can catch it, read it, and leave a reaction or an echo of their own.
