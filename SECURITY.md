## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability within Whispr2.0, please report it responsibly. 

* **Do not disclose publicly** until the issue has been addressed and a patch has been released.
* **How to report:** Send an email to [bloominbrandz@gmail.com] or open a private advisory on the [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/bloominbrandz/Whispr2.0).
* **What to include:** Please provide a description of the vulnerability, steps to reproduce the issue, and potential impact.

You can expect to receive an initial acknowledgment of your report within **48 hours**, followed by regular updates as we investigate and work on a fix.
